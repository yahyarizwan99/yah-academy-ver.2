How it works

Three steps, start to finished project

01 · Pick

Choose a path, not a playlist

Each path is a sequence of courses building toward one real outcome, so you always know what the next step unlocks.

02 · Build

Work in short, focused sessions

Twenty-five minute lessons, a project brief, and office hours with someone who's actually done the job.

03 · Ship

Finish with proof, not a certificate alone

Walk away with a project you can show, reviewed and signed off by a practitioner in the field.
