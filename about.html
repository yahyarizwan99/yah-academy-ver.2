About Yah Academy

We built the academy we wished existed.

Yah Academy started as a frustration with courses that end in a certificate and nothing else. We wanted something that ends in a finished project, honest feedback, and a mentor who's awake when you actually need one.

Our story

From a shared frustration to a working academy

Yah Academy was founded by three engineers and a writing teacher who kept hearing the same complaint from friends: online courses were easy to start and almost impossible to finish. Watching someone else code, or write, or model data, doesn't teach you to do it yourself.

So we rebuilt the format around one rule: every course ends with something you made, not just something you watched. Lessons are short. Projects are real. Mentors give notes the way a colleague would, not the way a script would.

Today that means hundreds of courses across development, data, writing, and product work, taught by people who still do the job day to day.

What we build toward

Proof, not just a certificate

A finished project you can show is worth more than a badge on a profile page.

Who teaches here

Practitioners, not narrators

Every mentor still does the work they teach, so feedback reflects what actually matters on the job.

How we measure success

Finish rate over enrollment count

We'd rather have fewer learners who finish than a large number who never come back.
