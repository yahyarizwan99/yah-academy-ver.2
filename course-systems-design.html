35 min

Most bad designs start from skipping this step.

  • Estimating scale before you design for it
  • Deciding what's explicitly out of scope

Module 2 · Data Storage Tradeoffs

60 min

Pick a database like the constraints actually demand.

  • SQL vs. NoSQL, past the marketing talking points
  • Partitioning and sharding without hand-waving
  • Project: justify a storage choice under load

Module 3 · Caching Without Creating New Problems

45 min

Caching solves one problem and quietly creates three others.

  • Cache invalidation strategies that actually work
  • Where a cache belongs in the request path
  • When caching is the wrong fix entirely

Module 4 · Scaling Reads and Writes Separately

50 min

Most systems don't scale symmetrically — design like it.

  • Read replicas and their real failure modes
  • Write contention and how to design around it
  • Load balancing strategies beyond round robin

Module 5 · Designing for Failure

55 min

Assume every part of your system will fail — because it will.

  • Timeouts, retries, and circuit breakers done right
  • Designing for graceful degradation
  • Project: design a rate limiter that survives a spike

Module 6 · Consistency and the Tradeoffs Nobody Wants

58 min

CAP theorem, but the version you actually use in an interview or review.

  • Strong vs. eventual consistency in practice
  • Picking the right guarantee for the actual use case
  • Explaining your choice without hiding behind jargon

Module 7 · Communicating a Design Under Pressure

40 min

A brilliant design that can't be explained doesn't get built.

  • Structuring a design doc reviewers actually read
  • Answering 'why not just...' questions calmly
  • Diagramming without overloading the page

Module 8 · The Live Design Review

50 min

Defend your design the way you would in a real review.

  • Handling pushback without getting defensive
  • Knowing which tradeoffs are worth defending
  • Final review session with your mentor panel
What you'll walk away with

A defended architecture for a system with real constraints

By the end of week ten you'll have designed, documented, and defended an architecture for a system under real scale, latency, and cost constraints — reviewed live by working engineers.

Final project: Design review defense

Due week 10

Design a system to a real-world brief (you'll pick from three: a ride-hailing dispatcher, a rate limiter for a public API, or a chat system at scale). Present your design in a live-style review and defend your tradeoffs against pointed questions.

  • Submitted through the course dashboard for mentor review
  • One round of written feedback before final grading
  • Passing projects earn a certificate and get added to your public profile
