Lesson 1 · Thinking Like an Analyst Module 1 of 8 · 30 min

Module 1 · Thinking Like an Analyst

30 min

Before touching data, learn to ask the question a dataset can actually answer.

  • Framing a business question you can measure
  • Spotting a dataset that can't answer it
  • Setting up a repeatable analysis workspace

Module 2 · Cleaning Data Without Losing Your Mind

50 min

Most of real analysis is cleanup — get comfortable doing it fast.

  • Handling missing values honestly, not just deleting rows
  • Catching duplicate and inconsistent entries
  • Project: clean a messy customer export

Module 3 · Exploring Before You Explain

45 min

Look at the data before you decide what story it tells.

  • Summary statistics that actually matter
  • Spotting outliers before they wreck your averages
  • Distributions vs. single numbers

Module 4 · SQL for Getting the Data You Need

55 min

Query real tables instead of waiting on someone to export them for you.

  • Joins that don't silently duplicate rows
  • Aggregating without losing the detail you need later
  • Project: answer five questions with one query each

Module 5 · Visualizing Without Misleading

48 min

A chart can lie by accident — learn to build ones that don't.

  • Picking the chart type the data actually calls for
  • Axis and scale choices that avoid distortion
  • Building charts people can read in five seconds

Module 6 · Finding the Story in the Numbers

40 min

Numbers alone don't convince anyone — the framing does.

  • Structuring an analysis like an argument
  • Separating correlation from your gut feeling
  • Project: write a one-page findings memo

Module 7 · Building the Dashboard

58 min

Turn scattered charts into one thing someone will actually open.

  • Layout choices that guide the eye
  • Filters and interactivity without overengineering
  • Performance: dashboards that don't crawl

Module 8 · Presenting to a Non-Technical Room

32 min

The best analysis fails if nobody understands the takeaway.

  • Leading with the decision, not the methodology
  • Answering 'so what' before anyone asks it
  • Final review session with your mentor
What you'll walk away with

A live dashboard built from a real, messy dataset

By the end of week eight you'll have cleaned, analyzed, and visualized four real datasets, ending with a dashboard you could hand to a stakeholder without apologizing for it.

  • Passing projects earn a certificate and get added to your public profile
